  <p>Appendix A</p>
  <h2>Definitions &amp; Methodology</h2>
</div>

<div class="content">
<div class="section">
  <div class="section-label">About this section</div>
  <div class="method-card">
    <p>This section explains what each indicator measures, how data is collected, its known limitations, and the data quality level assigned. It supports consistent interpretation across management and teams, and serves as reference for future external reporting.</p>
    <p>Data quality ratings — <span class="meta-pill high">High</span> <span class="meta-pill med">Medium</span> <span class="meta-pill low">Low</span> — reflect the reliability and completeness of the underlying data source at time of reporting.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section">
  <div class="section-label">Core activity type definitions</div>

  <div class="def-block">
    <div class="def-header"><div class="def-title">Awareness activity</div><span class="tb tb-s">Student-facing</span></div>
    <div class="def-body">An activity where the <strong>primary purpose is to create awareness</strong> of KU Lighthouse and its service offers. Awareness can technically occur at any event, but only counts here if it is the event's main objective.
    <ul><li><strong>Fairs</strong> — student-oriented campus fairs where LH represents itself with a booth (study start fairs, career fairs, student organisation fairs).</li><li><strong>Pop-ups</strong> — outreach events arranged by LH around campus areas to recruit for activities and create awareness.</li><li><strong>Social</strong> — activities across UCPH where LH contributes awareness-raising (e.g. KU Festival).</li><li><strong>Intro presentations</strong> — classroom presentations informing students about LH across UCPH faculties.</li><li><strong>Not counted:</strong> digital/online awareness, physical marketing (posters, flyers), or events where LH only provides location.</li></ul></div>
    <div class="def-meta"><span class="meta-pill">Source: Excel register (awareness activity data)</span><span class="meta-pill high">Data quality: High / Medium</span><span class="meta-pill">Owner: Sannie / Josefine</span></div>
  </div>

  <div class="def-block">
    <div class="def-header"><div class="def-title">Event</div><span class="tb tb-b">Both tracks</span></div>
    <div class="def-body">An activity where <strong>KU Lighthouse hosts or co-hosts</strong>. Events can be open to the public or closed. The event must have a <strong>sign-up for that specific one-off activity</strong>. If there is no digital pre-sign-up, the count is the number of people in the room. An event may span several days, but sign-up is a single registration — there is no application process. Events where KU Lighthouse only contributes with location are <strong>not counted</strong>.</div>
    <div class="def-meta"><span class="meta-pill">Source: Accelerace platform + manual Excel register</span><span class="meta-pill med">Data quality: Unknown / developing</span><span class="meta-pill">Owner: Lilian</span></div>
  </div>

  <div class="def-block">
    <div class="def-header"><div class="def-title">Programme</div><span class="tb tb-b">Both tracks</span></div>
    <div class="def-body">An activity where <strong>KU Lighthouse hosts or co-hosts</strong> a series of coherent activities running over time, targeted at <strong>the same cohort</strong> who engage in a series of learning activities. Participants go through an <strong>application process</strong> for the whole programme. The number of applications is <strong>counted in the quarter when programme admission takes place</strong>, not when the application round opens. Where programmes include participants from multiple universities, data is reported for the full programme (exception: Spin-Outs Denmark reports UCPH only).</div>

<div class="section">
  <div class="section-label">Breakdown dimensions</div>

  <div class="method-card">
    <h3>Faculty breakdown</h3>
    <p>All student-facing indicators are broken down by UCPH faculty where data permits: <strong>SUND</strong> (Health &amp; Medical Sciences), <strong>SCIENCE</strong>, <strong>HUM</strong> (Humanities), <strong>SAMF</strong> (Social Sciences), <strong>JUR</strong> (Law), <strong>TEO</strong> (Theology), and <strong>CROSS / UCPH Across</strong> for cross-faculty activities.</p>
    <p><strong>Limitation:</strong> Some study programmes span multiple faculties or universities (e.g. Bioinformatics: SUND/SCIENCE; Sundhed &amp; Informatik: KU/DTU). Participants are assigned to a single faculty based on best available data from the application.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="method-card">
    <h3>Academic level</h3>
    <p>Programme participants are categorised as: <strong>Bachelor/Master</strong>, <strong>PhD/Researcher</strong>, <strong>Graduate</strong> (postgraduate non-PhD), <strong>Other</strong>, or <strong>Not specified / Prefer not to say</strong>. Based on information provided during the application process.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="method-card">
    <h3>Gender</h3>
    <p>Four categories: <strong>Women</strong>, <strong>Men</strong>, <strong>Non-binary</strong>, and <strong>Not specified / Prefer not to say</strong>. Collected through the application process. Some historical programme cohorts did not collect gender data, resulting in gaps.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="method-card">
    <h3>Quarterly reporting logic</h3>
    <p>For <strong>programmes</strong>: applications counted in the quarter when admission takes place — not when the round opened. For <strong>events</strong>: attributed to the quarter of the event's start date. For <strong>researcher KPIs</strong>: reported when the transaction is formally recorded in Inteum, which may not exactly match the underlying activity date.</p>
  </div>
</div>

  <div class="section-label">Data systems referenced</div>
  <div class="method-card">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Inteum</strong> — KU's IP management system. Used for all researcher commercialisation indicators (IDFs, spin-outs, licensing agreements, innovation leads).</li>
      <li><strong>Accelerace platform</strong> — KU Lighthouse's community and programme management platform. Used for programme registration data, event sign-ups, student user counts, and startup tracking.</li>
      <li><strong>Gecko Booking</strong> — Booking system for logging intro meetings with students and researchers. Primary source for student lead data.</li>
      <li><strong>Excel registers</strong> — Manually maintained registers for awareness activities, curriculum teaching planning, InnoExplore applications, and selected event data.</li>
      <li><strong>Absalon / STADS</strong> — University course administration systems. Not currently used but identified as future options for improving student-in-course count accuracy.</li>
